# Business case

## 1. The idea

This product is for individuals who participate in hockey rec leagues and want a simple way to track their stats games and share that with the rest of the league. Most rec leagues today do not track any statistics and manually track games and if they do often times the games and stats are not available to the players. My product allows for a simple and easy way to track stats and games coming with a fully connected tracking program. All you have to enter the game information and it automatically populates stats like Goals, Assists, Points, Saves, Goals Against, Shutouts, Save Percentage, and Games Played. It also has a league schedule to help players see the games and when they are going to take place. This makes it so each game can be tracked and recorded and the rest is done for you. The company will make money by selling ad space on the website/app and selling a one time remove ad option for $1.49 and partnering with companies who want to connect to the hockey player demographic. This will make the product accessable to those who do not want to pay but also more convient to those who are willing while also creating a subtantial revenue source.

## 2. Where IT creates value

IT's main value comes in the form of accessable data integration so help ease the difficulties of running a rec hockey league. My product RinkLink gives hockey rec leagues a simple app to manage schedules, standings, and player statistics for their rec league in a conventient and automized way by automatically inputing game statistics into player profiles. It removes the need for manual tracking and makes all the league information available to every player. It automatically calculates save percentage and shutouts so you dont have to. It also determines based on the score of a game who won and updates the league standing including if it was an over time loss. The value in this product lies in its affordability, convenience, and better engagement for players. Once users start using this product they may be hesitant to switch do to switching costs such as saved player statistics, game scores, and formating the players, teams, and schedule. This product will also provide value to companies looking to market to the hockey demographic as by marketing on our platform through ads they will be targeting a very specific demographic of people. This could also lead to partnerships in the future with companies like custom jersey manufactorers, Graphic designers for team logos, etc. 

## 3. Network effects

My product does experience a network effect but only to a certain extent. The value of the network effect extends to the amount of people in your rec league that use the app / website as the more people who use it in your league the more connected the users are and the more value the product will provide however outside of that the product does not enhance the users expirence as more people use the product. An exception to this could be with more users and more revenue the products features could be improved and partnerships may also be implement in app like partnering with a jersey manufacturing company and allowing you to customize and order your teams jerseys in app this would add value to the product. For advertisers or potental partnerships the network effect does add value based on the amount of users as rec leagues often order custom jerseys so a partnership with a custom jersey manufacturing company or other companies targeting the hockey player demographic. This makes this market a two-sided because as the user base becomes larger the features will improve and in app partnerships will provide more features. As for the companies using the app to market to the hockey demographic a large user base will lead to more exposer leading to more value. To solve the empty network problem at launch I would proably present the product to whatever rec hockey league I was in and then use that league as a test population to refine the product and take suggestion for new features that they believe should be included. This would ad a small network population and allow me to enhance the product. From there I would probably try to spread the product through a social media platform and word of mouth to minimise marketing spending.

## 4. Can it be copied? (the moat question)

This app / website could be pretty easily replicated so the main differentation value would come from the roll out. This is because the product is currently being produced by a rec hockey league player (me) so it so I have a pretty good idea of what is important in a product like this but, I am only one person and we need the product to appeal to a large percentage of the rec hockey market in order to be successful so during the test / roll out period listening to the user base and implementing their recomended changes will be this products best advantage. This method of improving the product should be implemented even after a large customer base has been achieved. Another advantage after this product penatrates the market is the brand and switching cost. This is because the rec hockey market is relatively niche so after the app becomes an established product it will be hard for competitors to differenciate themselves enough to majorly errode our market share. This also means that as the product grows these advantages will strengthen over time. The switching costs will also be a large advantage as with our business model our product will be relatively inexpensive and with the low cost plus stored statistics and established league layout the switch cost to a different product would have to be almost free which isnt sustainable or provide very expoential benefits compared to our product. Simplicity and convience will also be a major business model built to differentiate us from competitors. This is because I believe that the rec hockey demographic prioritises easy use more than lots of features so the goal of the product is to provide the features that are the most important / desired while still being easy to use and convenient. For a competitor with a bigger budget who wanted to copy our product the app development would be one of their lower costs. The biggest cost would be trying to penetrate the already established market because the market is relatively inovation resistant as in most user cases simple is best. This is why it is so important for our product to capture a large market share fast as the product itself is easily replicable. So the bigger market share we capture the more difficult it will be to penetrate the market as a competitor. If a competitor wanted to errode our market share after we were established their main costs would be marketing through ads and other methods as an attempt to quickly create a customer base. Another method competitors could try and use is to optimise the user interface to make it feel like a much smoother product as our company would try to do this too but, with more money and reasources they would most likely be able to implement this method more effectively. Companies like Apple use this technique to mantain their market share.

## 5. Cloud and operations

This product would likely be offering SaaS (Software as a service) meaning we would store all the league data from each persons league. When starting out we would likely outsource this to a different company with databases already established but as the company grows we would likely build our own data centre to host the servers. This would also be beneficial as rec hockey leagues are most popular in colder climates and usually hosted during the winter months which would make it a beneificial to build our data centres close to our customers and make it cheaper to cool them as the majority of their use would be during the winter months. When starting out relying on rented cloud space may be risky as it could be expensive and we have no control over any network issues, potential data loss, or service outages. However this is why once a customer base has been established we will build our own data centres so that the service quaility is more in our control and in the cheaper in the long run but, until then rented cloud space is the best option as it allows us to scale at our pace and does not have any large inital costs.

## 6. Risks and ethics

Renting the datacentres of another company when starting out is risky as customers could lose data and the data centres may not be in close proxsimity to our user base lowing connection speed. To midigate this our company would try and buy cloud space from a reliable and relatively close source in order to reduce the chance of data loss and to try and midigate slow connection. Then once a large enough customer base has been achieved we will build our own data centre to host the app / website data ourselfs in an attempt to increase the proximity to our customers to have a more reliable connection and reduce the risk of data loss.
